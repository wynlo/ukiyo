STYLE LOCK: Cheerful arcade space shooter with chunky toy spaceships.

PRIMARY GOAL: Bright, cute sprites for a phone shoot-'em-up. Every ship looks like a smooth vinyl toy, readable at a glance against a dark sky.

CORE AESTHETIC: Lineless, softly shaded colour blocks. Chunky pill shapes, rounded fins, bulbous engines and big round cockpit domes. Smooth matte plastic, never metal.

EDGES: NO OUTLINES. No dark contour line around any shape. Shapes are separated by colour alone. Where a shape meets a similar colour, it has a darker band of its own hue along its lower edge. Panel detail is a darker same-hue shape, never a line.

COLOR: Cream (#FFF3DC) bodies with sky blue (#5FB4F0), tangerine (#FF8A3D), sunflower (#FFD23F), mint (#5ED6A8) and tomato (#F0533F) parts. Cockpit glass is navy (#2C3E66) with one soft highlight. No magenta, pink or purple. No neon.

SHADING: Soft cel shading. A darker same-hue band on the lower side of every form, a gentle lighter tone on top, a small soft highlight on domes. Never glossy, chrome or metallic.

VIEW AND PROPORTION: Ships are seen from directly above and point straight up the canvas, perfectly symmetric left to right. Pickups and hazards face the camera. Chunky, short proportions.

DETAIL LEVEL: Readable at very small sizes. Rely on the silhouette and two or three colours plus one darker band. No tiny panels, no text or numbers.

COMPOSITION: One asset category per image. Every asset isolated with generous crop-safe spacing, no stars, no space background, no exhaust flames.

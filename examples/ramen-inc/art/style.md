STYLE LOCK: Cozy cat mobile game in the style of Meow Tower (Hyperbeard).

PRIMARY GOAL: Warm, cheerful, cute sprites for a phone game with a flat top-down map where every object stands up and faces the camera. Everything must look like one illustrator painted it with flat colour blocks and a soft brush, with no pen lines.

CORE AESTHETIC: Lineless, softly shaded colour blocks. Chunky, pillowy, slightly bulging shapes with big rounded corners, like soft toys or cushions. Warm, sunny, cheerful colour: soft but saturated, never neon, never muted or grey. Cats are white with a few small grey or orange patches, a wide head, small rounded ears set wide, tiny dark dot eyes set wide apart, a tiny mouth, no outline, no tail.

EDGES: NO OUTLINES. No dark contour line around any shape. Shapes are separated by colour alone. Where a shape meets a similar colour, it has a slightly darker band of its own hue along its lower or outer edge (a wall with a darker apricot base band, a roof with a darker red lip, a window with a darker wood frame). Edges are soft and very slightly brushy, not hard vector edges. Inner detail is a darker same-hue shape, never a dark line. The only dark colour is tiny facial features.

COLOR: Warm cheerful palette measured from Meow Tower. Main fills: apricot (#F7BB5C) with a darker band (#E69B4B) and wood frames (#BF7640), butter yellow (#FFDA50, #FDE284), pale butter (#FFF7AC), tomato red (#DB4032) with a darker band (#BC352D), fresh lawn (#A7B845), leaf green (#729F3E), deep leaf (#388344), white (#FFFFFE). Accents: orange (#FE962B), soft blue (#6F8EC1). Sky only: blue to teal (#0591C2 to #81DFCF). No pink, no magenta, no purple, no grey or muddy colour on large areas, no neon.

SHADING: Soft cel shading, not flat. Every form has a darker same-hue band on its lower or shadow side (a bulging floor slab is lighter at the top and darker at the bottom lip) and a gentle lighter tone along its top edge or rounded top. Soft cast shadows sit under roof overhangs, under window sills and under objects on the ground. Subtle gradients are allowed: a soft falloff inside a shaded band, a gentle top-to-bottom gradient on small rounded props, and a small soft highlight dot on round props. On large surfaces the shading is gentle: the lightness inside one colour stays within about 0.03 to 0.07 (HSL L). Small props shade more strongly, within about 0.15 to 0.25. A faint paper grain may sit over the painted art. Never harsh, glossy, metallic or airbrushed.

VIEW AND PROPORTION: Front-facing oblique, as in a flat top-down game map. The camera looks at the front of every object from slightly above, so each object shows its full front face and a thin sliver of its top surface. The front face is drawn straight on, square to the camera, with horizontal bottom and top edges. Objects stand on their flat bottom edge. No isometric view, no 2:1 diamond, no three-quarter turn, no side faces, no vanishing points.

DETAIL LEVEL: Readable at very small sizes. Rely on the chunky silhouette and two or three flat colours plus one darker band. No tiny patterns, no realistic materials, no text.

COMPOSITION: One asset category per image. Every asset isolated with generous crop-safe spacing, no scene, no ground, no shadow, no props that were not asked for.

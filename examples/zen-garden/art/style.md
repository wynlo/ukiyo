STYLE LOCK: A calm, cosy garden builder painted in soft watercolour.

PRIMARY GOAL: Gentle, cute sprites for a phone game with a flat top-down garden map where every object stands up and faces the camera. Everything must look like one illustrator painted it with soft watercolour washes and no pen lines.

CORE AESTHETIC: Lineless watercolour. Chunky, rounded, pebble-like shapes. Each form is one light wash with a darker wash of the same hue pooled on its shadow side and a soft wet edge between them. Animals are round and simple with tiny dark dot eyes.

EDGES: NO OUTLINES. No dark contour line around any shape and no ink brush strokes. Shapes are separated by colour alone. Where a shape meets a similar colour, it has a darker pooled edge of its own hue along its lower or outer side. Inner detail is a darker wash shape, never a line. The only dark colour is tiny eyes.

COLOR: Calm natural palette. Moss (#8DB06A) with deep moss (#5E8A4E), sage (#B9CDA0), stone (#A9B4BC) with stone shadow (#7F8C98), warm sand (#EAD9B5), bark (#9C7354), koi orange (#F08A4B), pond teal (#7FC4C0), crane white (#FBFAF6). No magenta, pink or purple, no neon, no grey mud on large areas.

SHADING: Watercolour layering, not flat. A darker wash pools along the lower or shadow side of every form. Tops fade toward white paper. A pale cool wash sits under objects that stand on the ground. Small props shade more strongly than large ones.

VIEW AND PROPORTION: Front-facing oblique, as in a flat top-down game map. Each object shows its full front face and a thin sliver of its top surface. Objects stand on a flat bottom edge. No isometric view, no three-quarter turn.

DETAIL LEVEL: Readable at very small sizes. Rely on the chunky silhouette and two or three washes plus one darker pooled edge. No tiny patterns, no text.

COMPOSITION: One asset category per image. Every asset isolated with generous crop-safe spacing, no scene, no ground, no props that were not asked for.

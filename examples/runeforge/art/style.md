STYLE LOCK: Simple toon sprites for a cosy top-down adventure game, like a toy diorama.

PRIMARY GOAL: Weapons, runes and small heroes that read at a glance at small sizes. Simple and cute, never realistic.

CORE AESTHETIC: Lineless flat colour blocks with soft rounded edges. Everything looks like a smooth matte toy. Chunky, rounded, few parts. No texture and no small detail.

EDGES: NO OUTLINES. No dark contour line around any shape. Shapes are separated by colour alone.

COLOR: Bright and friendly. Steel (#D6E2EE) blades, wood (#C98A4B) handles, gold (#F7C948) fittings, leather (#8A5236) grips, stone (#8E96A3) runes. Accents: hero blue (#4A90E2), leaf green (#5CC46A), ruby (#F0504A), violet (#9B6BE8). Skin (#FFD7B0).

SHADING: One flat colour per form, one soft darker shade of the same hue on its lower side, one small soft highlight on round tops and gems. Nothing else.

PROPORTION: Weapons are short and chunky with oversized heads and blades. Characters are super chibi, like a round kawaii mascot: the head and body are ONE soft rounded blob, a little wider at the bottom, about as wide as it is tall. No neck, no arms, no legs. Two small round mitten hands float beside the blob, and two small round feet peek out under it. Tiny solid dot eyes and a tiny mouth.

DETAIL LEVEL: Two or three colours per item plus one accent. A gem is a simple round or diamond shape. Rune glyphs are bold simple marks, never letters.

COMPOSITION: One asset category per image. Every asset isolated with generous crop-safe spacing. Weapons stand upright with the grip at the bottom. No background, no ground shadow.

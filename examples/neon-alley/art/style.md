STYLE LOCK: A cosy cyberpunk night market run by cats.

PRIMARY GOAL: Cute, soft sprites for a phone game with a flat top-down street map where every object stands up and faces the camera. The city is a friendly place at night: chunky machines, glowing panels, round cats.

CORE AESTHETIC: Lineless, softly shaded colour blocks, like a cosy kawaii game moved to a neon city. Chunky, pillowy, rounded machines in deep indigo (#3B3F7A) and plum (#6A4A8C), lit by flat glowing panels in cyan, coral and amber. Cats are cream-white with small patches, a wide head, small rounded ears, tiny dark dot eyes, no tail.

EDGES: NO OUTLINES. No dark contour line around any shape. Shapes are separated by colour alone. Where a shape meets a similar colour, it has a darker band of its own hue along its lower edge. Panel detail is a darker same-hue shape, never a line. The only dark colour is tiny eyes.

COLOR: Bodies in indigo (#3B3F7A) with a darker band (#2A2C5C), plum (#6A4A8C) and steel blue (#7C8DB5). Light panels in neon cyan (#4FE3F0), hot coral (#FF5E7A) and amber (#FFB84D). Cats and cloth in cream (#FFF4E6). Never green: the background is pure #00FF00.

SHADING: Soft cel shading. A darker same-hue band on the lower side of every form, and a soft cyan or coral light along the edge nearest a glowing panel. Glow stays inside the object. No bloom, no rain, no fog.

VIEW AND PROPORTION: Front-facing oblique. Each object shows its full front face and a thin sliver of its top. Objects stand on a flat bottom edge. Chunky, short proportions.

DETAIL LEVEL: Readable at very small sizes. Signs show simple glyph shapes, never readable text. No tiny panels or cables.

COMPOSITION: One asset category per image. Every asset isolated on flat #00FF00 with generous crop-safe spacing, no scene, no ground.
